# Bright-Tv-Case-Study
This presentation analyses BrightTV viewership data to identify user behaviour, content performance, and viewing patterns. The insights highlight key drivers of engagement across content types, time of day, and geographic regions, and provide data-driven recommendations to support user growth and increase platform engagement.
To complete this Case Study i conducted my planning and design using Miro Diagram. I further used Canva to design my Gantt Chart graph wich further assisted me tracking my process by days. To be able to find the relevent information for my presentation and to be able to manipulate my data i then used Databricks. Further more i used Microsft excel to do my pivot tables and graphs. For my BrightTv Case Study presentation i used a Canva Generated design and edited with the data that i have conducted from my findings on databricks and excel.
